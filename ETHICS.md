# Research Ethics and Data Handling

This study analyzes only publicly available data: AI-authored pull requests,
the reviews they receive, and the comments posted on them, collected from
GitHub through the AIDev dataset (Li et al., 2025).

## Human participants

There are no human participants and no interaction with developers: the study
observes a public record of review activity. No informed-consent procedure
applies, and no institution-specific approval is required for research on
public data of this kind. The findings are reported in aggregate; the human
replies quoted in the paper carry pseudonymized names.

## Data handling

- **Text policy.** Review and comment text is not redistributed, with one
  exception: the role-coding worksheet carries the short human-reply bodies it
  labels, as they are needed to interpret the role codes. Third-party account
  names inside those bodies are replaced by pseudonyms, and the excerpts quoted
  in the paper are reproduced for research purposes with the repliers' names
  pseudonymized.
- **No personal data.** The released tables carry public GitHub identifiers
  only: repository slugs, pull-request and comment ids, and the public logins of
  the reviewing accounts. No names, email addresses, or other personal data are
  included.
- **Public identifiers.** Repository names remain public GitHub repository
  identifiers in the `owner/repo` format.
- **Terms of use.** Data was collected and is released in accordance with the
  GitHub Terms of Service and the terms of the AIDev dataset.
